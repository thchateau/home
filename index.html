Biography

Thierry Chateau is a professor at Université Clermont Auvergne and a member of Institut Pascal. He holds a PhD in Electronics and Systems (1999) and an accreditation to supervise research (2010). His work lies at the intersection of computer vision and artificial intelligence.

He directs FACTOLAB, a joint laboratory between Michelin, Université Clermont Auvergne and CNRS focused on the industry of the future. His activities combine academic research, technology transfer and teaching.
